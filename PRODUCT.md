# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Clinicians and clinical learners who need to look up sickle cell disease evidence quickly. They ask questions about the supplied guidelines and research sources, then verify the answer through page-level citations and retrieved passages.

## Product Purpose

Provide fast, grounded answers about sickle cell disease from the project's three-document corpus: the NHLBI 2014 expert panel report, the JCM 2024 analysis, and the WHO 2026 childhood guideline. Success means a user can ask a focused question and receive a useful answer with traceable source evidence, while unsupported or patient-specific requests are clearly refused or redirected.

## Positioning

This is a citation-bound clinical evidence assistant, not a general-purpose chatbot or diagnostic tool.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, calm, and rigorous. The interface should make evidence provenance visible and reduce uncertainty without creating false confidence.

## Anti-references

Do not make the product feel like a generic chatbot with playful chat bubbles or casual assistant language. Do not turn the first experience into a dense research dashboard full of metrics and technical controls. Do not present population-level evidence as personalized diagnosis, dosage, or treatment advice.

## Design Principles

- Put the answer first, then make its supporting evidence easy to inspect.
- Make provenance a first-class part of the response, not a footnote.
- Prefer clear boundaries over confident speculation when evidence is insufficient.
- Keep the primary workflow focused: ask, understand, verify.
- Use calm visual decisions that support careful reading under time pressure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard navigation, semantic structure, readable contrast, clear focus states, accessible form labeling, and reduced-motion alternatives. Preserve legibility for users reviewing dense clinical text and citations.
